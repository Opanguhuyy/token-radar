    How the ranking works

    Implied value = TVL × 0.25 × growth × patience × reach.

    • Growth — the protocol's 7-day TVL move, held inside −5% to +40%. A null move counts as flat.
    • Patience — 0.70 plus up to 0.30 for time listed with no token, topping out at two years.
    • Reach — 1 plus 0.04 per extra chain, up to four extras.
    • Board — the whole 40-protocol watchlist, plus every pre-token protocol under the $50M floor that is not a centralised exchange and not a versioned release: 226 rows.
    • Live tab — the 24 largest tokens by market cap, shown for contrast. They are not board rows: their token already trades.
    • Farm plans — seven steps per row: verify, set up a fresh wallet, do the core action weekly for four weeks, join the community, keep records, and watch for a token announcement. Gas only; no step promises a token.

    Data: the sealed snapshot as of . The board is the entry's own claim, never a promise from the protocol or the house.
